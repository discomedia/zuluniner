## Reproduce the article's limited model

Fixed aircraft cost: 4,500 holding + 1,500 acquisition + (60,000 purchase − 54,000 net sale) = USD 12,000.

Rental aircraft cost = 175 × H. Ownership aircraft cost = 12,000 + 85 × H.

- H = 70: rental USD 12,250; ownership USD 17,950; difference +USD 5,700.
- H = 100: rental USD 17,500; ownership USD 20,500; difference +USD 3,000.
- H = 140: rental USD 24,500; ownership USD 23,900; difference −USD 600.
- Crossing point: 12,000 ÷ (175 − 85) ≈ 133.3 hours, only with the other inputs fixed.
- Adverse 70-hour case: 17,950 + 3,000 extra repair + 1,200 extra holding + 2,500 lower net resale = USD 24,650.

Excluded from that model: taxes, financing, opportunity cost, major unplanned repairs and differences in instruction/other training costs. Add real inputs before making a decision.
