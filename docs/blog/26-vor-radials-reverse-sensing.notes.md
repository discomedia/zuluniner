## Original geometry, diagrams and study cases

The article uses a fictional station and stated magnetic reference. A position due east is on the 090 radial whether the airplane points east or west. For an ideal conventional receiver at that position, OBS 090 gives centered/FROM and OBS 270 gives centered/TO; heading is held independent in the four-row table. The off-course example holds a west-pointing airplane north of the 090 radial, changing only OBS: 270 gives TO/left, 090 gives FROM/right. South is to the left of the west-pointing airplane; following a right indication as a steering command with the reciprocal selected would take the airplane farther north.

An independent vector calculation checked centered cases, left/right signs, reciprocal flag changes and the mirrored west-side cases.
