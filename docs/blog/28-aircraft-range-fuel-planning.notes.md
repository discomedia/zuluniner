## Original calculations and worksheet

All fuel-budget quantities and performance in the exercise are explicitly fictional, not Skyhawk performance or a real dispatch plan. The baseline 160 L equals 4 L start/taxi + 12 L climb + 4 L descent/arrival + 10 L separate allowance + 30 L protected reserve + 100 L cruise. At the invented 40 L/h normal-cruise consumption, 30 L represents 45 minutes; the 100 L cruise allocation represents 2.5 hours. With a 200 km/h assumed still-air groundspeed it gives 500 km of cruise distance only. At a direct 40 km/h headwind that becomes 400 km. Starting with 130 L while holding all other allocations fixed leaves 70 L, 1.75 hours and 280 km at the assumed headwind groundspeed.

An independent arithmetic check verified all phase sums, reserve minutes, the 15-minute/10-L delay, each wind/loading result, NM conversions and rounded US-gallon quantities. The article explicitly excludes a total-trip-distance inference and an IFR-compliance claim from this simplified VFR ledger.
