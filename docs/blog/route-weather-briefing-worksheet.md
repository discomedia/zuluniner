# Route-weather briefing worksheet

Original ground-planning aid accompanying ZuluNiner's route-weather article. Not a completed briefing, legal-minimums calculator or flight authorization. Obtain current weather and aeronautical information for the actual flight and resolve questions with qualified support.

## Record the evidence

For every source, record the source URL/service, issue or observation time, forecast valid period, retrieval time, geographic/altitude coverage, and any missing or conflicting information.

## Make the decision traceable

- What remains unknown for each leg?
- Which evidence supports the planned corridor at the planned time and height?
- What condition or missing evidence requires postponement, cancellation or reassessment?
- Where/when will the plan be reviewed before alternatives narrow?
- Which alternatives are reachable, suitable and supported by their own current checks?
- Fuel implications of those alternatives, delays and detours:
- Latest update before departure; changes since the original briefing:
- Specialist/instructor questions and actual answers obtained (do not imply review):
- Decision, time, assumptions and next review point:

Keep the dated worksheet with its briefing records. Reassess when the route, timing, weather or aircraft/pilot circumstances change. A favourable airport colour or absence of a report doesn't fill a geographic gap.
