# Flight-review preparation and follow-up sheet

Original planning aid, prepared 22 October 2026 for US airplane pilots. This isn't an FAA form, approved syllabus, endorsement, dispatch checklist or substitute for the instructor's assessment. The instructor determines the review scope and satisfactory completion.

* Other qualification/recency requirements to check separately for the proposed flight:
* Aircraft model, configuration and equipment proposed for the review:
* Last flight, recent aircraft used and approximate recent flying activity:
* Types of flying completed recently, and conditions or tasks avoided:
* Long absence, equipment change or planned new mission:
* Instructor qualifications/familiarity and any separate checkout needed:
* Who will act as pilot in command, including any repositioning flight:

* What I want to do during the next period of flying:
* A specific decision or handling task I find difficult:
* A recent event I want to discuss:
* Records, current references and aircraft documents the instructor requests:
* Agreed scope, satisfactory-completion criteria, fees and arrangements for further training:

## Prepare a representative trip

* Proposed route, destination and alternatives:
* Current weather/charts/information used, with dates:
* Intended aircraft load and aircraft-specific performance data:
* Reasons to delay, divert, shorten the flight or choose a different destination:
* Effect of one changed input on the decision:
* Questions about airspace, navigation, systems or workload:

The instructor may choose a different flight plan or sequence. Don't use this sheet to prescribe unsupervised abnormal or emergency exercises.
