## Screen the weight

Takeoff limit minus documented empty weight minus any separately required operating load minus usable takeoff fuel = capacity for people and bags.

Subtract people and bags. A negative result fails the weight screen. A positive result still needs every check below. Use consistent units and unrounded inputs; round only displayed results.

## Reproduce the fictional article cases

All pounds below use invented empty weights that already include installed equipment, unusable fuel and full operating oil.

- A: 2,550 − 1,650 − (40 × 6) = 660 lb for people and bags.
- B: 3,400 − 2,000 − (60 × 6) = 1,040 lb.
- C: 3,400 − 2,150 − (60 × 6) = 890 lb.
- Four adults and bags: 4 × 180 + 60 = 780 lb.
- Five adults and bags: 5 × 180 + 60 = 960 lb.
- Six adults and bags: 6 × 180 + 60 = 1,140 lb.

The 40/60 US gal fuel quantities are chosen arithmetic inputs, not flight-planning recommendations. No CG or performance approval is provided by these examples.
