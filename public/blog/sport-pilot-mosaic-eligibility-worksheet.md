# Sport-pilot aircraft and flight screening worksheet

ZuluNiner original worksheet. Research checked 12 October 2026. This is a records checklist; completing it doesn't grant pilot privileges or determine medical qualification. Read the accompanying article and current FAA requirements. Copy one worksheet per aircraft and proposed operation.

## Aircraft records

* Registration, exact model and serial number: ____________________
* Airworthiness certificate/category and certification basis: ____________________
* Applicable flight manual, revision and supplements: ____________________
* Original certificated VS1 without lift-enhancing devices at maximum certificated takeoff weight and most critical CG, in KCAS; supporting page or record: ____________________
* Maximum seating capacity since original certification; supporting record: ____________________
* Cabin/pressurization status and supporting record: ____________________
* Power-loss directional-control and controlled-descent design evidence: ____________________
* Gear and propeller configuration: ____________________
* Alteration history and any unresolved original-eligibility questions: ____________________
* Other applicable §61.316 criteria and operating limits checked: ____________________

## Pilot and proposed flight

* Certificate, category/class and privileges to be exercised: ____________________
* Relevant training and endorsements verified with instructor: ____________________
* Medical qualification route confirmed; keep personal medical details in private records: ____________________
* Flight-review and passenger-currency status: ____________________
* Day/night and any night training, endorsement and medical conditions: ____________________
* Number of passengers, purpose, route/airspace, weather and altitude: ____________________
* Remaining restrictions affecting this flight: ____________________
* Actual loading, CG, fuel and performance calculations: ____________________

## Operator and insurer

* Rental/club authorization and checkout requirements confirmed: ____________________
* Insurer confirms pilot and proposed operation under actual coverage terms: ____________________
* Remaining documents, questions and person responsible for resolving them: ____________________

## Screening result

* Established facts, with document references: ____________________
* Unconfirmed facts: ____________________
* Next action before treating the aircraft as eligible or conducting the flight: ____________________

An ad title, empty passenger seat, lower current flight weight or aftermarket stall-reduction kit doesn't establish original aircraft eligibility. Check the aircraft, the pilot and the operation separately.
