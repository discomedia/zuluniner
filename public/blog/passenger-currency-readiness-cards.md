# Passenger currency and readiness — scenario cards

Original fictional ground-study companion to ZuluNiner article 31. Ordinary US private-pilot airplane cases; assume other applicable legal requirements are met except where a case identifies a gap. These cards don't approve a flight or establish instructor review. Check current rules and actual records for a real operation.

## Six scenario cards

1. **Flight review, one landing.** A satisfactory review last week included one takeoff and landing, with no other qualifying passenger-recency events. Identify the separate incomplete record before proposing a flight with a friend.
2. **Night label, uncertain times.** Three recent full-stop events are labelled night, but their times aren't established against the passenger-rule window. Identify the evidence needed; don't invent event times.
3. **Same class, different airplane.** Passenger recency is supported, but the next airplane has unfamiliar equipment and handling. Separate the regulatory record from the transition-training question and any other aircraft-specific qualifications.
4. **VFR itinerary becomes IFR.** Passenger recency is supported and the pilot is instrument rated, but instrument recency isn't supported. Identify the changed operating requirement and resolve it before the proposed IFR operation.
5. **Current, narrowly practiced.** Recent experience is only familiar daylight circuits; the proposed passenger trip ends somewhere unfamiliar after dark. Record the mission gap and a concrete instruction or itinerary change.
6. **Three months vs 90 days.** A fictional session on 1 August 2026 precedes a proposed 31 October 2026 flight by 91 days. Count dates rather than substituting calendar-month labels.
